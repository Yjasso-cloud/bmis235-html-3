Lip Liner

Fun Fact: E.L.F. has lipliners for 2 dollars

I love all the different combinations that I can get with lip liners. Similar to lash clusters, it can also change your whole look. If you go with a cool winter tone, then it darkens your face and complements different skin tones. When you do a warm summer tone, then it brightens your face and certain skin tones. For me, it makes my face warmer all around, so I tend to use warm colored lip liners.
