Lash Clusters

Fun Fact: Lash clusters are typically bonded and sealed. They can last up to 2 weeks depending on the bond you get and how well you maintain your lashes.

There are so many different lash clusters that you can choose from. Based on the style you get (spiky, manga, full, hybrid) and the curl pattern (150D, 200C, 100B, etc.), your entire makeup look changes. I also think as long as you have your lashes done, you always look somewhat put together, which is something I need when I pull up to class in my alpha wolf t-shirt and sweatpants with 2 hours of sleep.
